CITY GUIDE > BARCELONA

Barcelona Travel & Local Info

Weather

October in Barcelona brings comfortable autumn temperatures averaging 22°C – 25°C. Light jackets recommended for evenings.

Getting Around

Official taxis are black and yellow (FreeNow app works well). Metro Line L4 (Yellow) stops at Llacuna & Poblenou near campus.

Emergency & First Aid

General Emergency: 112. On-site First Aid Kits are available at campus reception.
